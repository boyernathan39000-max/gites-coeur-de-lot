We speak fluent French, Spanish and English. We live on site without disturbing you.

Nature all around you

A private pine wood with its own clearing, wide meadows where a doe and her fawn pass by morning and evening, lavender and wild flowers. No neighbours and no one overlooking you: just birdsong and absolute peace.

Two gîtes with natural air conditioning

Two 85 m² gîtes: you live at your own pace without bumping into anyone. The very thick stone walls keep things cool even in high summer: that is our natural air conditioning.

The ideal base

In the morning, visit Rocamadour 25 minutes away or Sarlat 50 minutes away. In the afternoon, enjoy the pool. In the evening, gaze at a starry sky of rare clarity with no light anywhere around. The A20 motorway is 10 minutes away.

Things to do

The finest sights in the region

You are less than an hour from the most beautiful villages, chasms, castles and restaurants of South-West France.

Villages and towns

Rocamadour, Sarlat, Saint-Cirq-Lapopie: the most beautiful villages around the gîtes

  • RocamadourThe sacred city clinging to its cliff, France’s second most visited site.25 min
  • Sarlat-la-CanédaThe highest concentration of listed historic monuments in Europe.50 min
  • Saint-Cirq-LapopieVoted France’s favourite village, high above the Lot valley.1 hr
  • La Roque-GageacOne of the Most Beautiful Villages of France, on the banks of the Dordogne.50 min
  • DommeA bastide town founded in 1281, one of the Most Beautiful Villages of France.50 min
  • Pont ValentréIn Cahors, the fortified 14th-century bridge and its three towers.40 min

Photo Gremi357, CC BY-SA 3.0

Caves and chasms

Padirac, Lacave, Pech Merle, Cougnac: the Lot underground

  • Gouffre de PadiracA shaft 35 metres wide and an underground river 103 metres deep.45 min
  • Grottes de LacaveChambers of rock formations to explore by little train.20 min
  • Grotte du Pech MerleA major site of cave art: 70 figures, 7 chambers and 1 km of galleries.50 min
  • Grottes de CougnacTwo caves near Gourdon: delicate rock formations and prehistoric paintings.25 min

Photo Benjamin Smith, CC BY-SA 4.0

Gardens and castles

Marqueyssac, Castelnaud, Beynac: the Dordogne valley and its fortresses

  • Jardins de MarqueyssacListed among France’s Remarkable Gardens.55 min
  • Château de CastelnaudA medieval fortress from the 13th to the 17th centuries overlooking the Dordogne.55 min
  • Château de BeynacOne of the best preserved and most famous castles in the region.1 hr
  • Parc animalier de GramatIn the heart of the Regional Nature Park, more than 1.000 wild and domestic animals.35 min
  • Forêt des SingesRocamadour’s zoological park on the Causse de Gramat.25 min

Photo Jebulon, CC0

Wine and truffles

  • Château de MercuèsAbove the Lot valley, the summer residence of the counts-bishops of Cahors for seven centuries.45 min
  • Château LagrézetteA château listed as a historic monument in Caillac, in the Cahors vineyards.45 min
  • LalbenqueOn the Causse de Limogne, the land of truffle oaks.50 min
  • Rocamadour cheeseRocamadour goat’s cheese in its home village.25 min
