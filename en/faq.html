Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before booking one of our gîtes with a pool in the Lot, near Rocamadour.

How many people can stay at Gîtes Cœur de Lot?

Each gîte sleeps 5 people in 85 m². Together, gîtes K and P form a 10-person gîte of 170 m² with three bedrooms. You can also camp in the pine wood if you are a larger group.

Is the pool private?

The 12 × 6 m pool is private to the estate: it is shared only between the two gîtes, in a green setting.

How far are Rocamadour and the sights of the Lot?

By car, Rocamadour is 25 minutes away, the Lacave caves 20 minutes, the Gouffre de Padirac 45 minutes, Sarlat-la-Canéda 50 minutes and Saint-Cirq-Lapopie 1 hour.

Do the gîtes stay cool in summer?

Yes. The very thick stone walls of the sheepfold keep the rooms cool even at the height of summer: it is the gîtes’ natural air conditioning.

Are the gîtes officially rated?

Yes, both gîtes are rated 4 stars.

How do I book direct?

Send your dates using the contact form. You can also write to Philippe on WhatsApp or by email, or call him on 06 80 42 17 52.
